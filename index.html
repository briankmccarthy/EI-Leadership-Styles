Build Your EI Matrix

For each leadership style, decide which emotional-intelligence competencies drive it. A signature star marks the competencies that are the engine of a style; a supporting triangle marks those that deepen or temper it. There's no answer key — this is your argument to make and defend.

How to use this tool

  1. Under Place a competency, choose ★ Signature or ▲ Supporting.
  2. Tap any cell to place that mark where the competency fits the leadership style.
  3. Tap a marked cell with the same tool selected to remove it, or use Clear all to start over.
  4. Your work saves automatically in this browser, so you can close the tab and come back to it.
